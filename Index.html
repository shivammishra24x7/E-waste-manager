About Us

Waste Manager ek aisi platform hai jo informal scrap collectors aur authorized recycling units ke beech ek digital pul (bridge) ka kaam karti hai. Humara uddeshya kachre ko surakshit aur transparent tarike se sahi jagah pahunchana hai taaki paryavaran (environment) ko bachaya ja sake aur collectors ko unki mehnat ka sahi aur uchit daam mil sake.
